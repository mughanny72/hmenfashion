Replace these two files:
1) admin.html
2) hmf-products-addon.js

Professional color variant setup:
- One product/style can have many colors.
- Every color can have its own image.
- Website color dropdown changes the image automatically.
- Cart saves the selected color and image.

Example:
Product Name: Slim Suit 2Pcs
Style Number: 2159
Color Variants:
Beige | 2159-beige.jpg
Olive | 2159-olive.jpg
Mint | 2159-mint.jpg

Open admin from:
http://localhost:3002/admin.html

Open website from:
http://localhost:3002/index.html
